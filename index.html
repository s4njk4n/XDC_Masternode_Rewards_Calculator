Things to know

It’s inferred, not read directly

The calculator compares your balance at the start and end of the period and removes everything your transactions explain. Whatever is left is counted as epoch rewards. Any other unexplained change in balance would be counted too.

What’s included

Native XDC only: normal and internal transfers in and out, and gas fees you paid. Failed transactions count their gas fee but not their value, since no XDC moved. Tokens aren’t included.

Dates follow your time zone

The time zone starts as your device’s. A financial year in Sydney runs from midnight Sydney time, not midnight UTC, and daylight saving changes during the year are allowed for. Choose UTC to match other tools that use it.

Your data stays with you

The page talks directly to the XDC RPC and Etherscan from your browser. Nothing goes to XDC Outpost. If you tick “Remember”, details are saved only in this browser.
